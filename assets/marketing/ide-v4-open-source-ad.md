## Primary ad (LinkedIn / X / landing)

### Headline
**Your IDE. Your repo. Your roadmap.**

### Body
Reports suggest major acquisitions are reshaping AI coding tools. If your favorite IDE just became someone else's product plan — you have another option.

**Neural Junkie v4** is an **open-source**, local-first agent IDE:

- **Full language-server depth** — gopls, rust-analyzer, pyright via Monaco LSP
- **Remote SSH workspaces** — `nj-remote` sidecar on your EC2 or dev box
- **Dev containers** — attach to `.devcontainer/` repos
- **Multi-agent workspace** — Backend, Security, Repo experts with **human approval on every file change**
- **Bring your own model** — Ollama on your machine, cloud APIs when you choose

We're not waiting for permission. We're building in the open. Fork it. Run it locally. Contribute a feature.

**Join us:** github.com/camronwood/neural-junkie
