# Walk Into a Room — Week 2 LinkedIn campaign

Sibling to [The Habit](../the-habit/THE-HABIT-LINKEDIN.md). Same seven-day rhythm. Different form: classic walk-into jokes. Dry. Short. The room is the product.

**Campaign:** Walk Into a Room  
**Series:** Week two  
**After:** Run The Habit Mon–Sun, then this the following Mon–Sun.  
**Format:** Organic LinkedIn feed posts. One still + joke per day.  
**Run:** 7 consecutive days. **8:30 AM ET** Mon–Fri. **10:00 AM ET** Sat–Sun.  
**Close (Day 7 only):** https://github.com/camronwood/neural-junkie/releases/latest

**Regenerate cards:**

```bash
./scripts/compose-walk-into-a-room-ads.sh all
```

## Day 1 — Monday

**Image:** `day-01-tabs-1080.png`  
**First comment:** “Week 2, 1/7 — Walk Into a Room. Tomorrow: an agent.”

### PASTE START

A ChatGPT tab, a Claude tab, and a Notes app walk into a room.

Nobody introduces anybody.

Someone has to paste. Someone always has to paste. That's the whole meeting.

I built a room where they don't need a translator.

### PASTE END

---

## Day 2 — Tuesday

**Image:** `day-02-agent-1080.png`  
**First comment:** “2/7 — The door has a lock. You keep the key.”

### PASTE START

An agent walks into a room.

It rewrites the files and leaves.

Nobody asked who invited it. Nobody saw the diff. The room just got rearranged while you were in Slack.

I don't want a guest that rearranges the furniture. I want one that waits for the key.

### PASTE END

---

## Day 3 — Wednesday

**Image:** `day-03-models-1080.png`  
**First comment:** “3/7 — Different brains. Same address.”

### PASTE START

Qwen and Kimi walk into a room.

They're both good. They don't share a desk.

One is local. One is rented. You wanted both without opening two apartments.

Different brains. One room. You name the seats.

### PASTE END

---

## Day 4 — Thursday

**Image:** `day-04-hats-1080.png`  
**First comment:** “4/7 — Specialists don't share a throat.”

### PASTE START

A generalist walks into a room wearing six hats.

Security still sounds like the backend. The code review sounds like the first draft. Everyone is polite. Nobody is specific.

I would not ask a plumber to sign the structural drawings. I don't know when we decided one chat window was every trade.

Specialists don't share a throat.

### PASTE END

---

## Day 5 — Friday

**Image:** `day-05-invoice-1080.png`  
**First comment:** “5/7 — Iterate local. Polish when it counts.”

### PASTE START

A draft and a review walk into a room.

Same invoice.

The messy thinking and the expensive pass should not share a bill by default. One of them can live under the desk. The other can show up when it has to.

Iterate local. Polish when it counts.

### PASTE END

---

## Day 6 — Saturday

**Image:** `day-06-argue-1080.png`  
**First comment:** “6/7 — Let them argue. You end the meeting.”

### PASTE START

Two agents walk into a room and agree immediately.

That's not a meeting. That's a mirror.

I want the person who will ship it and the person who will refuse it interrupting each other. I want fingerprints on the decision.

Let them argue. You end the meeting.

### PASTE END

---

## Day 7 — Sunday

**Image:** `day-07-you-1080.png`  
**First comment:** “7/7 — The room was the product. Come in.”

You walk into a room.

The models are already seated. The no is still yours.

That was the joke all week. Not another chatbot. A room.

Neural Junkie.

## Posting

1. Native image post. No URL on days 1–6.
2. First comment is the pin line only.
3. If someone asks what it is: “Desktop app. Local and cloud models in one room. You approve before files move.” Then stop.
4. Optional bridge from Week 1: Day 1 first comment can say “Last week was the habit. This week is the room.”

## Voice

Bar joke. Setup, beat, punch, one aftertaste line. Attack the feeling. Do not dump product nouns until Day 7 — and even then, one name and a door.
