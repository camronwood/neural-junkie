> I wanted my agents where I already work — **Slack on my phone**, not another tab I forget to open.
>
> **Neural Junkie beta.21** adds:
> - **Personal inbox** — DM the NJ bot; messages land in a private hub channel with the agent you chose
> - **Selective forwarding** — pull in `#eng` when someone `@`s you, you prefix with `nj:`, or you react with an emoji; replies stay in the **original Slack thread**
> - **Away mode (opt-in)** — when you’re marked away, labeled replies can go out in your 1:1 DMs via your user token (separate from the bot inbox)
>
> Desktop hub + specialists unchanged — Slack is another front door.
